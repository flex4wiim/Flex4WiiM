Android Permissions & Access

Reviewed version: 1.1.39 (version code 172)

Last updated: November 4, 2026

The following permissions are declared in the 1.1.39 Android manifest. Android may grant some automatically; runtime prompts and special-access settings depend on the OS version and feature. Declaring a permission does not mean it is continuously used.

Network access

  • INTERNET — WiiM control, local media-server communication, streaming sources and album artwork. Internet access is also the Android permission used for local-network sockets.
  • ACCESS_WIFI_STATE — Wi-Fi/network information used for local-device communication.
  • CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE — Multicast reception for local discovery, including compatible devices and UPnP/DLNA servers.

Local music

  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO — Audio-file access on Android 13 and later for My Music.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — Audio queries on older Android releases; declared with maxSdkVersion 32. This is a read permission, not permission to modify or delete the original music files.

My Music queries audio in the Android Music folder. Custom images use the Android system picker. The app does not declare READ_MEDIA_IMAGES or broad all-files management permission.

Alarms and background features

  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM — Scheduled Scene alarms; subject to Android's special-access controls and timing restrictions.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — Re-register saved alarms after startup. The receiver also handles app replacement and relevant clock/time-zone changes.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE — Ongoing services for supported background operations.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CONNECTED_DEVICE — Connected-device foreground services on applicable Android versions, including monitoring, music services and media controls.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — Playback controls, alarms, monitoring and service notifications; runtime notification access on applicable Android versions.
  • WAKE_LOCK — Keep necessary work active during supported operations. Optional screen-on behavior is also controlled by the app.

Battery-optimization settings can be opened for the user to review. The app does not silently grant itself an exemption. Alarm delivery and background work can still be delayed or interrupted by Android, the device manufacturer, or network conditions.

Other Android integration

The manifest queries launchable apps and WiiM Home for external-app shortcuts/integration. It does not declare QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES. The Quick Settings tile is protected by Android's BIND_QUICK_SETTINGS_TILE permission; the widget collection service is protected by BIND_REMOTEVIEWS. These are component access protections, not extra permissions requested from the user.

Permissions not declared

No microphone, camera, contacts, precise-location or approximate-location permission is declared. The analyzer does not use microphone recording or capture another app's audio output.

Managing access

Review the available controls in Android Settings under the app's permissions, notifications, alarms and battery settings. Disabling access may disable the corresponding feature. The app's JSON settings backup is explicit; Android automatic app-data backup is disabled.

For network destinations, local storage, exported data and voluntary feedback, see PRIVACY.md or privacy.html in this package.
